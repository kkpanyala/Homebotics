About Homebotics

Homebotics is a technology company that is focused on designing and developing commercially viable humanoid robots for household and domestic assistance. Homebotic’s main product is a safe, reliable, and human-oriented humanoid robot that can perform day-to-day activities in an average home, like helping with cleaning, cooking healthy, chef-level meals, retrieving items, doing the laundry, and other routine household tasks.

The Spark: Reclaiming the Modern Day

Every day, millions of hours are quietly lost to scrubbing floors, folding laundry, preparing meals, tracking down costly technicians, and worrying about the safety of our homes.

We live in an era of digital innovation, yet our physical lives remain anchored to tedious and repetitive chores.

Having spent years analyzing established global businesses, we recognized a massive, unaddressed gap in the market. Humanoid robotics existed in research labs but were not practical, nor were they accessible to the average household.

What if we could build an intelligent, safe, and truly affordable humanoid robots that give families their lives back?

Homebotics is more thna just a hardware company; it is a life-changing ecosystem. Through tailored monthly subscription packages, you can customize exactly what modules your robot learns. Whether you need a culinary chef today or a laundry specialist tomrrow, Homebotics has you covered.

Your time is your most valuable asset, and our top priority. Let us give it back to you. Welcome to Homebotics.
