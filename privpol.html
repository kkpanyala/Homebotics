Homebotics

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 17, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Homebotics. Homebotics is a C-Corporation humanoid robotics company dedicated to designing and developing commercially viable humanoid robots for household, domestic, and elderly assistance. We prioritize human safety above all else. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard user data when you visit our business website, purchase our humanoid robot models, or interact with our software services.

2. Information We Collect

To provide a human-centric experience and ensure our robots operate efficiently, we collect several categories of information through our hardware products, monthly software subscriptions, and web platform.

  1. Account and Subscription Information
    • Personal Identity Data: We collect your information when you register on our platform, purchase robot models (such as the Neo I, Phantom II, or Nova Sigma III), or request our services.
    • Subscription Package Details: We collect operational preferences tied to your monthly software subscription packages, such as dedicated modules enabling your robot to cook or do the laundry.
    • Financial Transaction Data: To manage the recurring revenue essential for our subscription ecosystem, we process billing details for software packages and hardware upkeep.
  2. Robot Operation and Environmental Data
    • AI/ML Spatial Processing: Our robots utilize advanced AI/ML pipelines to actively adapt to a variety of household environments. This requires the processing of environmental and telemetry data to execute domestic tasks safely.
    • Task Performance Logs: We collect data regarding specific repetitive chores performed by the robot, including cleaning, cooking, doing the laundry, and simple household repairs like fixing leaks.
    • Home Security and Surveillance Data: When configured as a smart security system to patrol your home against vulnerabilities, the robot processes data to detect unwanted intruders. In the event of an emergency, the system uses this data to conduct simple self-defense and immediately alert police officers.
    • Diagnostics and Upkeep Data: If you utilize our warranty and maintenance services, we collect operational diagnostics to fix malfunctioning units and optimize hardware longevity.

3. How We Use Your Information

We utilize the data we collect to deliver on our mission of empowering families to maximize their free time and helping the elderly live independently. Specifically, your data is processed to:

  • Deliver Autonomous Functionality: Ensure your robot operates safely and efficiently for its full operational window of approximately 5 hours on a single battery charge.
  • Maintain the Homebotics Ecosystem: Manage your active software accounts and streamline recurring customer services.
  • Improve Our Technology: Refine our advanced AI/ML pipelines and optimize our modular designs to keep our consumer options highly affordable.
  • Enforce Safety Protocols: Maintain active home security monitoring and execute emergency features that contact law enforcement when an intrusion occurs.
  • Support Commercial and Institutional Upkeep: Provide comprehensive technical support, diagnostic evaluations, and preventative maintenance for individual consumers as well as major institutions or facilities relying on our services.

4. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

We value your privacy and restrict the sharing of your personal data to operational necessities, including:
  • Emergency Response: Data is shared externally when the robot identifies a security breach and must immediately alert police officers.
  • Service and Maintenance: Telemetry details may be shared with technical teams deployed to manage warranty claims or fix malfunctioning robots.

5. Data Security and Longevity

Because Homebotics systems operate within intimate domestic environments, we implement robust security protocols to protect your information. Our structural framework is built to withstand internal transitions, ensuring that your data remains securely protected throughout the indefinite operational lifespan of the corporation.

6. Corporate Contact Information

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, you may reach out to the corporate owners found on the contact page.
